**Neon Tides** is an original open-world crime game set in Port Vireo, a neon coastal city at golden hour. You play Rook, a wheelman working off a debt to the fixer Vex: run a four-mission story chain (a dock pickup, a timed cross-town delivery, stealing the marked Solace coupe, and a three-star getaway to the beach hideout), then free-roam the city. Explore on foot or steal and drive cars through living traffic, dodge pedestrians, fight with a pistol, and stay ahead of the PVPD — a 0–5 star wanted system with chasing interceptor cars, officers on foot, BUSTED and WASTED outcomes. It features a procedural grid city with lit buildings and neon signs, a beach and ocean edge, a full HUD with a live canvas minimap, WebAudio-synthesized engine, siren, gunfire and ambient sound, and a localStorage save with Continue. Built with three.js (vendored), plain ES modules, no build step, fully offline.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

## How to play

- **WASD** — move on foot / drive · **Mouse** — orbit camera (click the game to pointer-lock) · **Shift** — sprint
- **E** — enter/steal the nearest car · **F** — exit car, or punch on foot · **Space** — handbrake
- **Mouse click** — fire the pistol · **R** — reload · **Esc** — pause (resume / save / restart)
- Follow the gold mission marker (also on the minimap), watch the wanted stars top-right, and break line of sight to let the heat decay. Progress saves automatically after each mission and from the pause menu; **Continue** on the start screen restores cash, mission and position.
